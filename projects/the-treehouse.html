The treehouse

A lookout on Ironbark stilts with a slatted bridge back to the hill, built for three kids and, the owners admit, mostly used by the adults.

Plan a build like this Call Ned
IronbarkEucalyptus paniculata
“You draw a treehouse the way a kid would, then you engineer it the way a carpenter should. The trick is not losing the first drawing.”Ned Harlow, from the shed diary

The story

The posts are Ironbark, set deep and braced so the platform does not move, even with a crowd on it. The roof frame went up over two days with a mate on the other end of every rafter.

The bridge slats are spaced close enough for small feet, the balustrade is higher than it needs to be, and there is not a single fixing where a hand will find it.
